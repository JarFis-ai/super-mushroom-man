Super Mushroom Man

A projector-first team quiz for ESL classes. The teacher runs it on one laptop and a projector; students answer out loud and never touch a device.

Play Super Mushroom Man Class Cookies

How a round works

A situation appears on the board, and the team says what they would say next. The teacher judges the answer. A right answer opens a block, and the block decides the reward: a payout of one to five coins, or a mystery block with one of seven outcomes, from doubling a team's total to swapping scores with another team.

  • 2 to 8 teams, on a board of 12, 16, 20 or 24 blocks.
  • Free to play with no login: three built-in sentence decks, each at three levels, 270 prompts in all.
  • An optional answer timer and sound.

How it was made

I designed the game and directed AI coding agents (Claude Code), which wrote the code; I set the rules and tests it has to pass, and I decide what ships. The game itself contains no AI.
